# CHURN REDUCTION INITIATIVE

## Executive Summary

### Situation

Customer churn is one of our largest revenue risks, currently costing the company approximately $2M annually. Our current churn rate is 7%, which is significantly higher than the industry benchmark of 4%. A review of customer behavior identified support response time as a major factor influencing customer retention.

### Key Findings

- **Support response speed directly impacts customer retention.** Customers receiving support responses within 2 hours have a 3% churn rate, while customers waiting more than 24 hours experience a 12% churn rate.

- **Current support response time is a major bottleneck.** The average response time is currently 6 hours, creating a gap between customer expectations and service delivery.

- **High-value customers are most affected by slow support.** Customers spending more than $10K annually show significantly higher churn when support delays occur.

### Business Risks

- **Revenue Loss:** Current churn levels result in approximately $2M annual revenue loss. Reducing churn represents a significant opportunity to recover lost revenue.

- **Customer Value Risk:** High-value customers are more likely to leave when service quality declines. Losing major accounts creates long-term revenue impact.

- **Competitive Risk:** Customers experiencing poor support may move to competitors offering faster service and better customer experience.

- **Operational Risk:** Increasing support demand without additional resources increases employee workload and may reduce service quality.

### Recommendations

1. **Increase Support Capacity**
   
   Hire two additional support engineers to reduce average response time from 6 hours to under 2 hours. Expected impact is $400K in recovered revenue within the first year.

2. **Introduce Response Time Standards**

   Establish a company-wide support response target of under 2 hours. Track performance daily to improve accountability.

3. **Create Priority Support for High-Value Customers**

   Introduce a dedicated support process for customers spending more than $10K annually to reduce high-value customer churn.

### Decision Needed

Approve a $200K support hiring budget and allocate $50K engineering resources for support improvements. The expected return is $400K recovered revenue in the first year.

### Next Steps

- Operations team finalizes hiring and SLA plan.
- Finance reviews budget approval.
- Recruitment begins support engineer hiring.
- New support process becomes operational within the planned timeline.
